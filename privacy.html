Privacy Policy

ARIA Apply AI is an AI-powered Chrome extension that assists users with completing online job applications.

Information We Process

  • Resume information selected by the user.
  • Form fields on job application websites opened by the user.
  • Candidate profiles stored locally in the browser.

Data Storage

Candidate information is stored locally using Chrome Storage. AI requests are sent only to the configured backend for resume parsing and semantic mapping.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is processed only to provide the requested functionality.
